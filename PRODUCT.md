# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript

## Users

Engineering students, alumni, industry professionals, corporate recruiters, and academic staff.

## Product Purpose

To showcase the annual ENSAM-RABAT Forum, highlight its history, present the current edition's theme and schedule, and facilitate connections between engineering students and industrial partners for internships (PFE/PFA) and recruitment. Success means high engagement from both students and companies, leading to successful networking and recruitment outcomes.

## Positioning

The premier platform connecting future engineers from ENSAM-RABAT with the Moroccan industrial sector, focusing on innovation for the Morocco of tomorrow.

## Operating Context

Used by students preparing for their careers, companies looking to recruit top engineering talent, and alumni staying connected with the institution. Often accessed during the build-up to the annual forum events.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Needs to look modern, dynamic, and premium to attract top-tier companies and engage students.
- Must present schedules, historical timelines, and participating companies clearly.
- Built as a React Single Page Application.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Forum ENSAM-RABAT
- Theme (Current Edition): "L'INGÉNIEUR AU CŒUR DE LA TRANSFORMATION INDUSTRIELLE : INNOVER POUR LE MAROC DE DEMAIN"
- Visual Identity: Dark mode aesthetic with vibrant, glowing accents (pink, cyan, gold) that convey a sense of high-tech innovation and premium quality. Fonts include Orbitron (headings) and Montserrat (body).

## Evidence on Hand

- Historical data (timeline of events from 1978 to 2026).
- Presentations schedule for 2024 (Schneider Electric, Wavestone, Forvis Mazars, VINCI ENERGIES).
- Background video asset (`/Forum Ensam Rabat.mp4`).
- General presentation content of the school (from `extracted_pdf2.txt`).

## Product Principles

1.  **Professional yet Bold:** Balance the professional needs of corporate recruiting with the dynamic, forward-looking energy of an engineering school.
2.  **Clarity of Information:** Ensure event schedules and historical context are easily scannable and accessible.
3.  **Visual Excellence:** Employ modern web design techniques (glassmorphism, micro-animations, glowing effects) to create a memorable first impression.
